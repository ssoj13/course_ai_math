Every scene is a small DCC viewport: a header (view type, projection, Overlays, Reset view), the dark viewport with
the navigation gizmo (3D) or the XY corner (2D), the N-panel of draggable number fields (press `N` over the card or

Drag b to point exactly opposite a: the angle arc becomes a semicircle and the label reads 180 degrees. Drag b onto
the origin: the arc and its label disappear (a zero vector has no angle) and nothing else breaks. Turn on the

bar. At the starting values the angle is acute, so the correction is negative: the bar grows inward and is hatched.
Drag b past 90 degrees and it turns positive and plain.

## Lesson primitives

Vertex drags with custom mappings: B slides along the x axis and sets the length a; A moves in polar terms and sets b
and the angle, which sticks to 30, 45, 60, 90 degrees and the other ticks on the arc. Arrow keys on a focused vertex
step it and the vertex glides to the new value while the numbers jump. The ledger under the card is the same equation
as an area balance: at 90 degrees the correction vanishes and only a thin line remains.
